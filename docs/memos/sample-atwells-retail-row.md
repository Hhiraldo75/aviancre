## 8. Key risks and mitigants

- **Over-rented roll (+31.5% vs market).** Income steps down as leases roll. *Mitigant:* bid on marked-to-market income ($3.55M max bid) and confirm the $22.00/SF street-retail market rent with signed comps on Atwells Ave.

- **Casa Lucenti's $30.00/SF option.** The tenant can renew above the projected $23.35/SF market, or leave a 3,400 SF restaurant space. *Mitigant:* the model already assumes renewal at market; get the tenant's sales and the restaurant fit-out condition to judge the odds of renewal.
- **Vacant suite 200 (25.0% of SF).** Year 1 carries $59,400 of absorption & turnover vacancy and $108,000 of TI. *Mitigant:* broker leasing plan and LOI pipeline for the space before closing.
- **Rent growth below expense growth.** The engine shows NOI falling during the hold at 2.0% rent growth or less. *Mitigant:* verify the NNN recoveries and the $100,800/yr operating expense estimate against actual bills.
